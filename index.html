The Five Evidence Tabs

Read a Listing Clue in Five Tabs

A spreadsheet row is a clue, not a verdict. Sort what you see into five tabs before you decide anything about an item.

SSource

Identify where the clue actually came from — the shared Sheet, a category page, or the real target page you opened.

  • A Sheet row is a pointer, not proof.
  • Prefer the clue closest to the target page.

PPage identity

Check the page you really landed on: its title, its domain, and where the link finally resolved.

  • Confirm the destination matches the item you meant to inspect.
  • Redirects can change the page silently.

IImage context

Understand what stage an image belongs to, and what that image cannot prove.

  • Listing photo ≠ what you will receive.
  • A QC snapshot proves only that one unit, at that moment.

OOption context

Re-read color, size, version, quantity, and price against the option you have selected right now.

  • A price may apply to only one variant.
  • Quantity changes what the price means.

?Unknowns

Keep a list of what is still missing, conflicting, or waiting for confirmation on the target page.

  • Shipping, stock, and exact version often stay open.
  • An open question is not a resolved answer.

↳Why the order matters

Source, then page, then image, option, and unknowns. That sequence keeps a clue from being mistaken for a confirmed listing.

Listing Clue Boundary

What a Listing Clue Is — and Is Not

✓A listing clue IS

  • A pointer toward a target page.
  • A community-compiled reference row.
  • A starting point for your own review.
  • A saved share link to revisit later.

✕A listing clue IS NOT

  • A guarantee about the item.
  • Live inventory or stock status.
  • QC verification or a saved record.
  • An endorsement by this resource.

Stop Rules

When to Stop and Re-verify

These signals mean a clue is not ready to act on. Stop, and resolve the gap first.

  • 1

    Page identity mismatch. The opened page’s title or domain does not match what the clue described. Stop and re-check the link.

  • 2

    Option conflict. Color, size, version, quantity, or price no longer lines up with your selection. Stop and re-read the options.

  • 3

    Image gap. There is no image context for the actual item you are reviewing. Stop before assuming anything.

  • 4

    Unresolved unknown. Shipping, stock, or the exact version is still unclear. Stop until the target page confirms it.

  • 5

    Untraceable source. The clue cannot be traced back to the Sheet or a category page. Stop and locate the source first.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

A JoyaGoo spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. It is a collection of clues, not a record of verified items.

Is this website the official JoyaGoo service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by JoyaGoo or Google.

Do the evidence tabs verify a product or confirm quality?

No. The five evidence tabs are a reading framework for a listing clue. They help you separate a source clue, the current page, image context, option context, and open unknowns. They do not provide live monitoring, product verification, QC certification, or a purchase guarantee.

How do I use the JoyaGoo spreadsheet to browse categories?

Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references, then use the category links on this page to browse a grouped catalog. Treat every row as a starting clue and confirm the target page before ordering.

What should I check before relying on a listing clue?

Confirm where the clue came from, verify the page you actually landed on, understand the image stage, re-read the selected option and price, and keep a list of anything still unknown or conflicting.

Where do I place an order after reviewing a listing?

After reviewing a listing clue on the target page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item for processing. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
