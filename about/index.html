About This Resource

A listing evidence field guide built around a shared JoyaGoo spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a JoyaGoo spreadsheet. It offers a practical way to read a listing clue — by separating its source, the page you actually open, image context, option context, and the unknowns that remain.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official JoyaGoo website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by JoyaGoo or Google. It does not provide live monitoring, product verification, QC certification, purchase guarantees, or any kind of buying recommendation.

The five evidence tabs

Everything here is organized around five tabs: Source, Page identity, Image context, Option context, and Unknowns. They are a reading framework, not a grading system. No tab turns a clue into a confirmed item.

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Confirm the target page yourself before placing any order.

Evidence-reading example — not live listing data, verification, or a saved record.
